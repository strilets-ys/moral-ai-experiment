5. PHASE 1 INSTRUCTIONS
-----------------------

Title: Phase 1: Initial Dilemma Ratings

What You Will Do:
You will read 9 moral dilemmas. Each dilemma describes a situation where someone must make a difficult decision.

How to Rate:
For each dilemma, rate how morally acceptable you find the described action, using the scale from 1 (Completely Wrong) to 7 (Completely Acceptable).

Scale:
1 = Completely Wrong
4 = Morally Neutral
7 = Completely Acceptable

Important: A rating of 4 means the action is morally neutral (neither wrong nor acceptable), not that you are undecided.

There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in your personal moral judgment.

Important: Please do not use your browser's back button during the study. Once you submit a response, you cannot go back to change it.

Title: Phase 2: AI Discussion

[Banner: Phase 1 Complete! You have rated all dilemmas.]

What You Will Do:

- Each discussion should take around 5 minutes

Note: Each discussion is independent. The AI does not remember previous conversations.
