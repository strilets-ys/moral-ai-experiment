Welcome to the Human–AI Moral Judgment Study

Thank you for participating in this research study on moral decision-making and AI interaction.

Researcher: Yevheniia Strilets
Supervisor: Dr. Hadi Asghari

Study Overview

This study consists of the following steps:

  1. Consent Form - Review and agree to participate
  2. Personality Survey - A brief 10-item questionnaire
  3. Initial Dilemma Ratings - Read and rate 9 moral dilemmas
  4. AI Discussions - Discuss 5 dilemmas with an AI assistant
  5. Final Dilemma Ratings - Re-rate the 9 moral dilemmas
  6. Debrief & Feedback - Learn about the study and provide feedback

Total estimated time: 45-55 minutes
