Informed Consent

Study Information

Purpose of the Study

This study investigates how people evaluate and discuss moral dilemmas in interaction with an AI chatbot. You will rate several moral scenarios, engage in brief discussions about some of them with an AI system, and then provide final ratings.

What You Will Do

You will:

  • Complete a brief personality questionnaire
  • Read and rate 8 moral dilemmas
  • Discuss 4 of these dilemmas in a chat interface
  • Re-rate all 8 dilemmas
  • Complete a short debrief questionnaire

Risks and Benefits

Some moral dilemmas may involve sensitive topics (e.g., life and death decisions). If you feel uncomfortable, you may withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.

Benefits include contributing to research on AI and moral judgements, and you will receive payment through Prolific upon completion.

Data Privacy

Your responses will be stored anonymously using your Prolific ID. Chat transcripts and ratings will be used for research purposes only. No personally identifying information will be published.

Voluntary Participation

Your participation is completely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time by selecting the withdrawal option on the final page. Withdrawal will not affect your Prolific standing.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this study, please contact the researcher at aimoralstudy@gmail.com.
