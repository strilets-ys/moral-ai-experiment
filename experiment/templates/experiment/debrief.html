About This Study

Thank you for participating in this research on moral decision-making and AI interaction. This study investigates how AI-assisted discussions may influence moral judgments.

The AI you interacted with was programmed to present counterarguments to your initial positions. This was designed to explore how exposure to different perspectives affects moral reasoning.

Your responses will help us understand the potential effects of AI on human moral judgments, which is important for the responsible development of AI systems.
