Study Debrief

About This Study

Thank you for participating in this research on human–AI interaction and moral judgment. This study examined whether brief discussions with an AI chatbot can influence individuals' evaluations of moral dilemmas.

During the study, the AI was instructed to take a position on each dilemma - sometimes agreeing with your initial rating to reinforce your view, and sometimes disagreeing to challenge it. In some conditions, the AI was also provided with your demographic information and/or personality profile to tailor its responses. These variations allowed us to examine how different interaction settings affect moral evaluations.

Your responses will contribute to research investigating how AI systems may shape moral reasoning and decision-making. Understanding these effects is important for assessing both the opportunities and risks of increasingly persuasive AI technologies.
